Privacy Policy for inboxCRM

Effective date: September 4, 2026

inboxCRM ("the Extension", "we", "us", "our") is a browser extension that adds customer-relationship-management tools to WhatsApp Web. This policy explains what information the Extension handles, where it is stored, and the limited circumstances in which any of it leaves your device.

The Extension is published by Extino Ltd.

1. Summary

  • The Extension is local-first. Your CRM data — pipelines, notes, tags, templates, schedules, orders and reports — is stored in Chrome's local extension storage on your own computer.
  • We operate no server that receives your WhatsApp messages or CRM records.
  • Information leaves your device only when you choose a feature that requires it: the optional AI assistant, optional webhooks you configure yourself, an optional self-hosted bridge server you run, and — when account sign-in is enabled — our account and subscription service.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We run no analytics or telemetry inside the Extension.

2. Information the Extension handles

2.1 Data stored locally on your device

Stored in chrome.storage.local and never transmitted by us:

  • Contact names and phone numbers (WhatsApp chat identifiers) attached to the CRM records you create
  • Kanban boards, stages, deal values, tags, categories and custom tabs
  • Notes you write about contacts
  • Quick-reply templates and any images, video, audio or documents you attach to them
  • Scheduled messages, appointments, follow-up rules, chatbot flows and auto-reply rules you configure
  • Order and delivery records, and any custom contact profile fields you add
  • Campaign recipient lists and send logs
  • Sending-safety settings and the counters that enforce your daily and hourly limits
  • Interface preferences (language, theme, privacy blur)
  • Backups you export or schedule

2.2 WhatsApp message content

To provide its features, the Extension reads content from the WhatsApp Web page you have open — the currently selected conversation, its participants, and recent message text. This is used to display the panel, to evaluate the automation rules you set up, and, if you use the AI assistant, to give the AI model context. The Extension does not create an archive copy of your chat history; only the CRM records you create are stored.

2.3 Credentials

  • Any AI provider API key you choose to save
  • The access token for the optional self-hosted bridge server
  • When account sign-in is enabled: your Extino session token and cached subscription status

All are stored locally. API keys are sent only to the provider they belong to.

3. When information leaves your device

3.1 AI assistant (optional)

If you use the AI assistant to draft or improve a reply, the Extension sends a prompt to the AI provider you selected. That prompt contains message text from the conversation you have open (by default the most recent 15 messages), the contact or group name, and anything you typed in the composer.

Depending on your settings the recipient is one of:

  • OpenRouter — openrouter.ai
  • OpenAI — api.openai.com
  • Google Gemini — generativelanguage.googleapis.com
  • Groq — api.groq.com
  • Your own proxy server — the self-hosted bridge described in the Extension's documentation, which forwards the request to OpenRouter using a key held on your server

Each provider processes that data under its own privacy policy and terms. We do not receive a copy. If you never use an AI feature, no message content leaves your browser.

If you enable per-message AI rewording in bulk campaigns, the message text for each recipient is likewise sent to the provider you selected.

3.2 Account and subscription (api.extino.net)

When account sign-in is enabled in your build, the Extension communicates with our account service at https://api.extino.net to sign you in, read your profile, check your subscription status (re-checked in the background roughly every six hours), retrieve pricing, and open Stripe checkout or billing-portal sessions.

Data handled for this purpose may include your user ID, email address, display name, profile image, session token and subscription state. No WhatsApp message content and no CRM record is ever sent to this service.

Payment card details are collected by Stripe on Extino-hosted billing pages. They never pass through the Extension, and we do not store them.

3.3 Webhooks you configure

The Extension can POST event notifications to URLs you enter in its Webhooks screen. Events you can subscribe to include incoming messages, quick replies sent, auto-attendance triggers, follow-ups dispatched, schedules fired, CRM updates, label changes, completed conversations and Meta-ads clicks.

Depending on the event and the fields you enable, a payload may contain the contact's phone number, their name, and the message body — and, for the incoming-message event, optionally downloaded media. You choose the destination and are responsible for its security and for whatever lawful basis you need to send that data there. We never receive these payloads.

3.4 Self-hosted bridge server (optional)

If you run the optional bridge server and turn it on in the Extension, the Extension's background worker connects to the address you entered (for example http://localhost:3900 or a tunnel URL) and accepts send commands relayed through it. That server runs on infrastructure you control. We do not operate it and receive nothing from it.

4. How information is used

Information handled by the Extension is used only to:

  • Display and organise your WhatsApp conversations inside the CRM
  • Store the notes, tags, templates, pipelines and records you create
  • Send the messages you compose, schedule or approve
  • Apply the sending-safety limits you configure
  • Generate AI drafts when you request them
  • Deliver the webhook events you subscribed to
  • Verify your subscription, when sign-in is enabled

We do not use it for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, or sale to any third party.

5. Third parties

Third party Receives When
OpenRouter, OpenAI, Google (Gemini), Groq AI prompt containing recent message text and your draft Only when you use an AI feature
Extino account service (api.extino.net) Account identity, session token, subscription status Only when sign-in is enabled
Stripe (via Extino-hosted pages) Payment details you enter on their page Only on purchase
Endpoints you enter in Webhooks Event payloads you configured Only for events you subscribed to

Each processes data under its own policies. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone not listed above except where required by law or to protect users and the service.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Local data stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove individual records inside the Extension, export an encrypted backup, clear the Extension's storage from chrome://extensions, or uninstall the Extension, which removes its local storage entirely.
  • Back up first if you want to keep your records. Use the Extension's Backup screen to export before uninstalling; the exported file is yours to keep or delete.
  • AI providers retain prompts according to their own policies. Review the policy of whichever provider you configure.
  • Account records, where sign-in is enabled, may be retained for account, billing, fraud-prevention, legal and support purposes. To request access to or deletion of that account data, contact extinoltd@gmail.com.

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Practical steps you should take:

  • Keep your AI provider API key private, and prefer a key with a spend limit.
  • Only send webhooks to endpoints you control and trust.
  • Do not expose the optional bridge server to the public internet without authentication.
  • Store exported backup files somewhere safe; they contain your CRM data.

8. Your choices

You can, at any time:

  • Use the Extension without configuring AI, webhooks or the bridge server
  • Remove a saved API key from Settings
  • Turn off individual automations and the AI assistant
  • Export, import or delete your data from the Backup screen
  • Sign out, where sign-in is enabled
  • Uninstall the Extension, removing all locally stored data

9. Children

The Extension is a business tool and is not directed to children. It is not intended for anyone under the age required to consent to online services in their jurisdiction, and we do not knowingly handle such a person's data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Extension changes. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. Material changes will be reflected in the Extension's Chrome Web Store listing and, where appropriate, announced in the Extension itself.
